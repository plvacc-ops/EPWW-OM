Local aerodrome regulations​

Due to the lack of published departure procedures from EPZG airport, departure instructions should always be coordinated with APP Poznań. If no further air traffic control is logged after departure, the flight crew should be instructed to continue the flight with their own navigation (“Po odlocie z własną nawigacją”/ “After departure resume own navigation”).

Datalink clearance​

Datalink clearances at the Zielona Góra airport are not available.

Ground movement procedures​

Apron 1 – can be used for handling large transport aircraft, up to code F. Apron 3 – dedicated to civilian, commercial aircraft.

VFR traffic​

During high traffic load, aircraft in VFR flight may be instructed to hold on one of the published holdings: — on the north side of the aerodrome NOVEMBER, — on the south side of the aerodrome SIERRA.
