## Local aerodrome regulations

### Datalink clearance

Datalink clearances at the Lublin airport are **not available**.

### Ground movement procedures

Stand 11 can be used as holding position for category D aircraft.
Stand 10 is used for deicing.

### Aircraft DE-ICING

De-icing is possible only on stand no. 10 with engines turned off.

### VFR traffic

During high traffic load, aircraft in VFR flight may be instructed to hold on one of the published holdings:
— on the north side of the aerodrome **TANGO**,
— on the south side of the aerodrome **SIERRA**.

### Low Visibility Procedures (LVP)

LVP operations will be commenced when any of the RVR falls below 550 m and/or the cloud ceiling falls below 200 ft. 

Pilots are informed about LVP being in operation by radio, with use of following phraseology: *“Low visibility procedures in operation. CAT II in operation”*. 
If weather conditions does not allow CAT II approaches the following phraseology should be used instead: *“Low visibility procedures in operation. CAT II not available”.*

LVP will be terminated when all RVRs increase to 600 m or above and ceiling rises above 200 ft and a continuing improvement is anticipated.

|Phase|RVR|Cloud ceiling or VV|
|-|-|-|
|Preparation|≤ 800 m|≤ 300 ft (90m)|
|LVP|< 550 m|< 200 ft (60m)|
|Suspension|≥ 600 m|≥ 200 ft (60m)*|

During LVP for departure and landing only runway 25 should be used. Runway 07 can be used for departure on pilot request. After landing runway should be vacated via taxiway A and reported to ATC.

Minimum RVR for departure is 125 m.

|-|-|

|Arrival (RWY 25)| ≥ 300 m (CAT II)|
