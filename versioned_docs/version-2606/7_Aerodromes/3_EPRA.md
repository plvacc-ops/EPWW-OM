## Local aerodrome regulations

### Datalink clearance

Datalink clearances at the Radom airport are **not available**.

### Ground movement procedures

EPRA_M_GND is responsible for military part of the aerodrome maneuvering area during civil
tower work. The rest of maneuvering area (civil) is controlled by EPRA_TWR.

Civil part of maneuvering area contains of:
- taxiways: A, A1, A4, B, D, E;
- runway 07-25.

|TWY|RESTRICTION|REMARKS|
|-|-|-|
|D, E|36 m|-|
|A5, A6, M|24 m|MIL ACFT ONLY|

### VFR traffic

VFR traffic passing through CTR/MCTR Radom or performing aerodrome traffic circuits should fly at altitude not exceeding ***3000 ft** AMSL.

In case of high air traffic intensity, an aircraft performing a VFR flight may receive, if necessary,
a holding instruction over the following points:
- **SIERRA** at altitude of 1500 ft AMSL,
- **HOTEL** at altitude of 2000 ft AMSL,
- **ROMEO** at altitude of 1500 ft AMSL on the northern side of national road no. 12,
- **NOVEMBER** at altitude of 1500 ft AMSL.
